PolicyLens · provisional inference

Comment stance & sentiment analysis

PolicyLens studies how people respond to Canadian federal Open Government consultations. This page runs the project's saved TF-IDF baseline models directly on text you enter below. Results are provisional suggestions, not ground truth — the training labels were AI-generated working labels, not human-verified.

Critical limitations — must read before use

  • Standard model: outputs Support, Neutral, or Unclear only — it cannot predict Oppose.
  • Heuristic Oppose option: a separate, documented rule (negative sentiment + non-Support stance) can flag Oppose, but it is NOT a validated Oppose classifier — see the disclaimer shown with its results.
  • Training labels are AI-generated; they are not human-verified ground truth.
  • Decision margins are not probabilities and are not calibrated confidence scores.
  • A previously recorded failure classified a strongly opposing statement as Support.
  • Models were trained on ~400 Canadian consultation comments; coverage of other domains/languages is unknown.
  • Sentiment (tone) and stance (position) are distinct constructs; do not conflate them.
  • Reported evaluation scores do not establish human-validated accuracy.
  • Input text is processed in memory only and is not stored by this endpoint.
